Privacy Policy

MoneyPot is built to keep your financial data private.

Last updated: September 11, 2026

MoneyPot is an iOS app that helps you track personal expenses, income, and net worth. We designed it to keep your financial data private.

📱 What we store

All expense, asset, recurring, and category records are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. The app does not transmit these records to any server we operate.

☁️ iCloud sync (opt-in)

If you enable iCloud sync in the app's Privacy Choices sheet, your records are synchronized to your personal iCloud account using Apple CloudKit. The data is encrypted at rest and in transit, and remains under your Apple ID. MoneyPot does not have access to your iCloud-synced data.

🤖 AI scanning and category suggestions (opt-in)

When you grant consent and use the camera scanning feature, the captured photo is sent to our AI service provider (OpenAI via AIProxy) solely for the purpose of extracting the receipt or product details. Images are not stored on our servers and are not used for training.

With your AI consent, category suggestions can also send entered expense titles and category information to OpenAI via AIProxy. Batch categorization can include expense titles, amounts, current categories, and merchant details when present, and is used only to suggest appropriate expense categories.

✉️ Monthly summary emails (opt-in)

If you enable "Email me a monthly summary" in Edit Profile, we send a verification email to the address you provide and queue summaries only after you confirm it. To provide the service, our backend processes your email address, language preference, subscription and verification identifiers, and the summary prepared by your device (month, spending totals, category breakdowns, asset names and values, and net worth). It does not upload a copy of your entire local database. Cloudflare Workers and D1 host the subscription and delivery queue; Resend sends the emails; Firebase App Check verifies requests from the app. Turn the toggle off or use the unsubscribe link to stop future summaries and delete the server-side subscription.

📊 Analytics & diagnostics

MoneyPot uses a few third-party services to understand how the app is used and to keep it stable: Firebase Analytics (Google) collects anonymous, aggregated usage events such as which screens are opened, using the ad-identifier-free variant that does not collect the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites; Firebase Crashlytics (Google) collects crash and diagnostic reports so we can fix bugs; and Adapty manages in-app subscriptions and purchases. These services may process a device-scoped identifier and interaction data linked to your app installation. None of it is used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and MoneyPot does not use App Tracking Transparency.

✉️ Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact apputopiacy@gmail.com.

In short: your records live on your device (and, if you choose, in your own iCloud). We don't run a server that collects them, and we don't sell or share your data.
